# RadixDB trademark policy

The RadixDB name, wordmark, and logos identify the official RadixDB project and
its distributions. This policy does not state that any mark is registered.

The software licenses do not grant permission to use RadixDB branding in a way
that suggests sponsorship, endorsement, or official status.

You may use the RadixDB name truthfully to describe compatibility, dependency,
or origin, provided the use does not create confusion. Modified distributions
and forks must use a distinct name and branding and must clearly state that
they are not official RadixDB releases.

Do not use RadixDB names or logos:

- as the primary name or logo of another database product or service;
- for a domain, company, package, or hosted service that appears official;
- to imply endorsement or a commercial relationship without written
  permission.
