# Code of Conduct

[Русский](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ru.md)

RadixDB uses a simple rule for project communication: be direct, technical and
respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal worth.
- Explain trade-offs with evidence where possible.
- Assume database bugs can be serious; report them clearly and calmly.
- Keep discussions welcoming to people who are learning the codebase.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Intentionally derailing security or data-corruption reports.
- Pressuring maintainers to hide known correctness or durability risks.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate issues, discussions or contributions that violate this
code. Repeated or severe violations may lead to temporary or permanent loss of
participation privileges in project spaces.
