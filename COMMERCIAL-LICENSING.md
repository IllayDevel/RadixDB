# Commercial licensing

PolyForm Perimeter 1.0.1 already permits use of RadixDB for noncompeting
purposes, including internal business use and use as infrastructure inside a
product that does not compete with RadixDB.

Contact the licensing steward before using RadixDB to provide a competing
database product or service, including:

- a rebranded or minimally modified RadixDB distribution;
- an OEM database distribution;
- a competing fork offered as a product;
- RadixDB or a compatible derivative offered as DBaaS or a managed database.

Commercial permissions are granted only by a separate written agreement.
